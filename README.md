# ProjectNinja

## Overview
2D Infinite Vertical Scroller where you are a ninja trying to escape a collapsing temple.
Pieces of the temples roof constantly fall on you, meanwhile a swarm of ninjas tries to stop you from escaping
You must use your ninja tools at your disposal to dodge, weave and ultimately escape

## Design
The player traverses a infinite scrolling temple, while the score incrementes based on the meters travelled.
The player will have to dodge projectiles, enemies in a timely fashion because the camera is slowly raising from the bottom.
If the player touches the limit, either the top or the bottom one, game over.
The player has at their disposal, regular movement(run, jump), double jump, grappling hook and a means to defend himself.
